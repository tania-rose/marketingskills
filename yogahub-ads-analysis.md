## Agency Identification (from naming conventions)

Three distinct naming patterns reveal different agency/operator involvement:

### 1. "GROW//" — Agency managing Alchemy of Breath

Naming pattern: `GROW//[Product]//[campaign-type]`

Examples:
- `GROW//Breathwork Facilitator Training//leadgen`
- `GROW//Webinar2503//leadads`
- `GROW//Breath The World//leadgen`
- `GROW//BreathCamps//retargeting`

**Strategy:** Webinar funnel + lead ad campaigns for breathwork training programs. Uses a clear hierarchical naming convention with product and campaign type separated by `//`.

### 2. "[TG]" — Agency working across Alchemy of Breath AND Infiniti Fitness

Naming pattern: `[TG] [Product/Event] - [Type]`

Examples on Alchemy of Breath:
- `[TG] - BreathCamp 2026`
- `[TG] - Quantum Shift Event - Leads`
- `[TG] Breathwork - Webinar - Prospecting`
- `[TG] Seeds Of Alchemy - Leads`

Examples on Infiniti Fitness:
- `[TG] Free Training | HIIT Classes`
- `[TG] Reformer Challenge | Leads | Infiniti Fitness`
- `[TG] Personal Training | Leads`
- `[TG] Reformer | Infiniti Fitness`
- `[TG] COLD | MORRISTOWN | GRAND OPENING`

**Strategy:** Event-based and product-specific lead generation. Uses `[TG]` prefix consistently across multiple accounts. Focuses on direct sales and lead campaigns.

### 3. BuilderBee / In-House — NBD Fitness campaigns

Naming pattern: `[Program] - [Location] - [Version]` or `[Program] - [Radius] - [Type]`

Examples:
- `Fit Over 60 - Morristown - V2`
- `Fit Over 50 - Morristown - V3`
- `Reformer 40 - 5 Mile - No Booking`
- `TS 40 LGT - 5 Mile - No Booking`
- `Fit Over 60 - Morristown - V3 - Leads to BuilderBee`

**Strategy:** Age-segmented local fitness programs with geographic radius targeting. "Leads to BuilderBee" indicates leads routing through BuilderBee's system.

### 4. Structured Agency — Infiniti Fitness formal campaigns

Naming pattern: `Infiniti Fitness | [Type] I [Detail] [[Brand]]`

Examples:
- `Infiniti Fitness | Leads I PT | LG [[Infiniti Fitness]]`
- `Infiniti Fitness | Leads I WC [[Infiniti Fitness]]`
- `Infiniti Fitness | Leads I LG I GLP [[Infiniti Fitness]]`

**Strategy:** Professional structured naming with brand tagging. Focuses on lead gen with website conversion and lead form variants.
