## QUICK TIPS FOR YOUR FIRST CLASS

### Section Headline
> First time? Here is what to know.

**Arrive 5 minutes early**
Give yourself time to settle in. Late arrivals are not permitted and may count as a late cancellation.

**Bring water and a towel**
Especially for hot classes. You will want both.

**Wear whatever is comfortable**
Leggings, shorts, a t-shirt — whatever lets you move. No special gear needed.

**Do not worry about being new**
Every single member started exactly where you are. Instructors cater to all levels and will help you find modifications if needed.

**Cancellation reminder**
Need to cancel a booking? Do it at least 6 hours before the class start time to avoid a €5 fee.
