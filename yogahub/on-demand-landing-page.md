## CLIENT BRIEF (Context for Wording)

This page replaces the current `/start` flow. The previous version of the page caused customer confusion about the 7-day free trial converting to a paid subscription — which has been leading to refund requests and chargebacks.

**Every piece of copy on this page — especially anywhere near a CTA — must make the following unambiguous:**

1. The trial is **free for 7 days**.
2. After 7 days, the card on file **will be automatically charged** €12.99/month or €129/year (whichever plan they chose).
3. **Cancellation is the customer's responsibility.** They must cancel themselves before day 7 to avoid being charged.
4. The customer must actively **acknowledge (via a required checkbox)** that they understand and accept the auto-renewal terms before their card is captured.
5. **Refund requests are reviewed case by case.** Once cancelled, no further charges occur.

Do not rely on T&Cs alone. The billing terms appear in the hero, at every CTA, next to the checkout checkbox, and in a plain-English pre-footer summary.

## OBJECTION HANDLING / FAQ

### Section Headline
> Questions? Sorted.

**Q: Will I be charged during the 7-day free trial?**
A: No. You will not be charged during the 7-day trial. Your card is only charged on day 8 if you have not cancelled.

**Q: What happens on day 8?**
A: On day 8, if you haven't cancelled, your card will be charged the price of the plan you selected at checkout — €12.99 for the monthly plan or €129 for the annual plan. Your subscription then renews automatically each month or year.

**Q: How do I cancel?**
A: You cancel yourself in your account settings under **"Manage Subscription."** You can cancel any time before the end of day 7 and you will not be charged. You can also cancel at any point after that to stop future renewals.

**Q: Why do you need my card details if the first 7 days are free?**
A: So that your subscription can continue seamlessly if you choose to keep it. We're upfront about this: on day 8, you will be charged the plan price unless you cancel first. No hidden conversion — it's the whole offer, stated clearly.

**Q: Can I get a refund if I forget to cancel?**
A: Refund requests are reviewed case by case. Once you cancel, no further payments will be taken. If you believe you were charged in error, contact support and we'll review your account. Please note — by checking the acknowledgement box at checkout, you confirm that you understand and accept the auto-renewal terms.

**Q: Do I need any equipment?**
A: A mat is useful for most classes. Some classes suggest light props (a block, a strap, a towel) but most can be done with just you and the floor.

**Q: What if I already have a YogaHub studio membership?**
A: On Demand is a separate subscription. Studio members get a discounted rate — contact your studio to add On Demand to your membership instead of starting a new subscription here.

**Q: Can I share my account?**
A: Your subscription is for one person. Please don't share your login. If you'd like to gift YogaHub On Demand to someone, gift cards are available.
