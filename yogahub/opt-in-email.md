# YogaHub — Intro Offer Purchase Confirmation Email

**Email Type:** Transactional / Welcome (Triggered on purchase)
**Goal:** Confirm purchase, get them to book first class, build excitement, tee up membership upsell
**Tone:** Warm, direct, encouraging — like a friendly studio manager, not a corporate brand

---

## Email 1: Purchase Confirmation + Welcome

**Trigger:** Immediately after 2 Week Unlimited Intro Offer purchase
**Subject Line Options:**

- Option A: `You're in — your 2 Week Pass is live`
- Option B: `Welcome to YogaHub. Let's get you booked.`
- Option C: `Your 14 days start now — here's what to do first`

**Preview Text:** Book your first class across any of our 8 studios. No limits. No catch.

---

### Email Body

---

**Hey [First Name],**

Welcome to YogaHub. Your 2 Week Unlimited Pass is active and you've got 14 days of classes ahead of you.

Here's the quick rundown:

**Your Pass Details:**
- **Pass:** 2 Week Unlimited Intro Offer
- **Price:** €49
- **Valid from:** [purchase date]
- **Valid until:** [purchase date + 14 days]
- **Studios:** All 8 YogaHub locations
- **Classes:** Yoga, Mat Pilates, Barre, Hot Classes

---

**Book your first class now.**

The hardest part is the first one. After that, you'll be hooked.

[**BOOK A CLASS**] ← Primary CTA button (links to timetable)

Browse the timetable, pick a class, and lock in your spot. You can book as many classes per day as you want — there's no limit.

---

**A few things to know before your first class:**

- **Arrive 5 minutes early.** Late arrivals aren't permitted and may count as a late cancellation.
- **Bring water and a towel** — especially for hot classes.
- **Wear whatever's comfortable.** No fancy gear required.
- **All levels are welcome.** Instructors offer modifications so you can go at your own pace.
- **Need to cancel?** Do it at least 6 hours before the class starts to avoid a €5 late cancellation fee.

---

**What's not included:**

This pass doesn't cover reformer pilates. Everything else — yoga, mat pilates, barre, hot classes — is yours to try.

---

**What happens after the 2 weeks?**

Nothing automatic. No surprise charge. Your pass simply expires.

But if you love it (and we think you will), you'll get an exclusive code for **20% off your first two months** of any YogaHub membership. We'll send that towards the end of your pass.

---

Go book something. Your mat is waiting.

— The YogaHub Team

---

**Fine print:**
Your 2 Week Unlimited Intro Offer is non-transferable and non-refundable. Valid for 14 days from the date of purchase across all YogaHub studios. Includes yoga, mat pilates, barre, and hot mat-based classes. Does not include reformer pilates. Cancellations within 6 hours of the class start time incur a €5 fee.
