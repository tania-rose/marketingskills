## CREATIVE DIRECTION NOTES

See `ad-creative-briefs.md` for full visual specs. Key points for Meta:

- **Image ads:** Studio photography of real classes in session (not stock). Show the energy of the room. Diverse bodies, real sweat.
- **Text overlay:** Keep under 20%. Lead with price point: "€49 / 2 WEEKS / UNLIMITED"
- **Video ads (15-30 sec):** Hook in first 3 seconds — show a packed class, instructor adjusting someone, people laughing after class. End with offer card.
- **Stories/Reels:** Vertical (9:16). Native feel. Quick cuts between class types. End with swipe-up CTA.
- **Carousel:** One card per class type (Yoga / Pilates / Barre / Hot). Final card = offer CTA.
