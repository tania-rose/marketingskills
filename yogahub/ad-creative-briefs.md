# YogaHub — Ad Creative Briefs

**Purpose:** Visual direction and specs for ad creatives supporting the 2 Week Unlimited Intro Offer and Membership Promo Code campaigns.

---

## Brand Direction (Working — Pending Brand Guidelines)

**Note:** No formal brand guidelines file was found. The direction below is based on YogaHub's positioning as a multi-studio yoga/pilates/barre brand in Dublin. Update these when brand guidelines are confirmed.

**Tone:** Warm, energetic, real. Not corporate wellness. Not Instagram-perfect. Think "your favourite studio" — welcoming, a bit sweaty, full of real people.

**Photography style:** Real classes, real people, real sweat. No stock photography. No posed "yoga on a cliff at sunset." Show the studio, the instructors, the community.

**Colour direction:** Defer to YogaHub brand colours. If unavailable, use clean/neutral backgrounds with bold text overlay.

**Typography:** Clean, modern sans-serif. Bold for headline text overlays. High contrast for readability.

## CREATIVE SET 2: Intro Offer — Video Ads (15-30 seconds)

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Photography Needed
- [ ] Wide shots of each class type (yoga, pilates, barre, hot) during live classes
- [ ] Instructor-student interaction moments (adjustments, encouragement)
- [ ] Post-class community moments (chatting, laughing, water breaks)
- [ ] Studio exteriors for at least 3-4 locations
- [ ] Close-ups: hands on mat, feet at barre, focused faces, sweat
- [ ] At least 2-3 diverse class groups (age, body type, gender)

### Video Needed
- [ ] 2-3 hours of b-roll across at least 3 studios and 4 class types
- [ ] At least 1 full class filmed for cut-down options
- [ ] Natural audio captures (instructor cues, background ambience)
- [ ] Post-class candid moments

### Design Deliverables
- [ ] Static ads in all required dimensions (see specs above)
- [ ] Video ads edited to 15s and 30s cuts
- [ ] Carousel cards (5x 1080x1080)
- [ ] End cards / offer cards for video
- [ ] Animated version of price hero (optional — for higher CTR)

### A/B Testing Priority
1. **Creative concept** (price hero vs. class type split vs. objection buster) — biggest impact
2. **Headline/hook** (first 3 seconds of video, headline on static)
3. **Image style** (wide class shot vs. close-up vs. instructor interaction)
4. **CTA copy** on end cards
